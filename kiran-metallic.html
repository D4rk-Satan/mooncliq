Kiran Metallic: 4.5x Faster Invoicing and Dispatch with Zoho Books

How a Surat-based industrial product hub, trusted since 1985, set up Zoho Books for speed and compliance, from GST e-invoices to dispatch.

Industry

Location

Zoho Books setup

The story in 60 seconds

From manual paperwork to one compliant, connected finance flow

Before

A multi-segment industrial supplier serving roofing, HVAC, insulation and construction customers had to keep invoicing, compliance and dispatch moving together:

●Invoice and e-way bill details re-typed for every dispatch

●Purchase orders and dispatches handled as disconnected steps

●Cash inflows and outflows visible only after they had happened

What we built

Zoho Books, set up for speed and compliance:

●1-click GST e-invoicing: compliant e-invoices generated straight from Zoho Books

●Automated e-way bills: created for dispatches without re-typing

●Cash-flow forecasting: expected inflows and outflows visible before they hit

●Faster purchase orders and dispatches: a connected flow from order to delivery

After

A faster, more accurate and fully visible finance operation:

●4.5x faster invoicing, purchase orders and dispatches

●99.8% accuracy in financial reconciliation

●Real-time visibility of cash-flow and accounts as they happen
