Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Zoho CRM pricing, deployment timelines, and API integrations.

Does Zoho POS work without internet connectivity? ▾
Yes! Cashiers can scan items, apply discounts, and print receipts completely offline. Data synchronizes back to the cloud as soon as connection is re-established.
What hardware devices are supported? ▾
Zoho POS supports standard ESC/POS thermal printers, USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners, cash drawers, and card payment terminals.
Will our sales team find it difficult to transition to Zoho CRM? ▾
No. We configure Zoho CRM to match your existing naming conventions and workflow stages so your reps feel right at home. We also conduct interactive walkthroughs and provide custom screen recordings.
Do you offer ongoing support after go-live? ▾
Yes, every implementation includes 30 days of complimentary post-deployment support. We also provide monthly retainer packages for continuous Deluge development and workflow optimizations.
