FAQ

WhatsApp Automation FAQs

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. We are a Meta Tech Provider and set up the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API.

Do customers need to opt in?

Yes. WhatsApp requires customer consent for business messages, and we set up opt-in flows that follow its policies.

Can I keep my existing number?

Often yes, but the number must be eligible for the API. We check it and guide the migration.

What does it cost?

Costs include Meta messaging charges and our setup fee. We explain both in your quote.

Which countries is it best for?

It works worldwide and is strongest in India, the Middle East, the UK and Europe. In the US it is usually an add-on channel.
