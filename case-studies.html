Kiran Metallic

Industrial Products & Steel Infrastructure
Zoho Books Cash Flow Forecasting

The Challenge

Established in 1985, Kiran Metallic supplies industrial steel, roofing, and HVAC products to giants like L&T, Adani, and Reliance. Rapid enterprise growth caused major operational friction:

  • ✦ Slow and manual accounting workflows delaying multi-warehouse dispatches.
  • ✦ Lack of real-time cash flow visibility and predictive financial forecasting.
  • ✦ Time-consuming manual GST filing, e-Way bills, and multi-vendor ledger reconciliations.

MoonCliq Solution

MoonCliq architected an end-to-end financial ecosystem powered by Zoho Books and automated ledger pipelines:

  • ✦ Customized Zoho Books setup with 1-click GST e-Invoicing & automated e-Way bill generation.
  • ✦ Predictive cash flow forecasting dashboards configured for executive decision-makers.
  • ✦ Automated vendor credit terms, aging analysis, and payment reminder schedules.

Measurable Results

The transformation completely removed accounting latency, giving leadership transparent control over financials:

  • ✦ 4.5x Faster daily invoicing, purchase order processing, and warehouse dispatches.
  • ✦ 100% Real-time cash flow visibility and accurate monthly revenue forecasting.
  • ✦ Zero manual errors across multi-category tax audits and compliance filings.
"MoonCliq restructured our financial operations with Zoho Books. Accounting which used to take days is now completed in real-time, giving us complete clarity over our cash flow and upcoming financial forecasting." — Abhishek Mehar, Director, Kiran Metallic

The OZ Studio

Mumbai, India • Commercial CGI, VFX & Production Studio
Zoho CRM </> Custom Web Application

The Challenge

As a high-end CGI and VFX studio in Mumbai crafting ad films for leading FMCG & luxury brands, managing multi-tier agency relationships was critical:

  • ✦ Fragmented customer data spread across scattered spreadsheets and email chains.
  • ✦ Lack of automated follow-ups and stage-wise project status tracking for clients.
  • ✦ Difficulty in identifying high-intent repeat contracts, resulting in churned revenue streams.

MoonCliq Solution

MoonCliq engineered a synchronized enterprise CRM framework paired with a custom web application:

  • ✦ Configured customized Zoho CRM blueprints mapped to creative production lifecycles.
  • ✦ Developed a bespoke Custom Web Application to manage client briefs, revision approvals, and billing triggers.
  • ✦ Automated customer re-engagement sequences and milestone triggers to nurture ongoing client retainers.

Measurable Results

The integrated CRM and custom app empowered The OZ Studio to deliver seamless high-touch agency experiences:

  • ✦ 38% Increase in repeat client retention and multi-project retainer renewals.
  • ✦ Automated Client Workflows: Zero dropped project briefs and prompt revision turnarounds.
  • ✦ Diversified Revenue Streams: Clear visibility into client lifetime value (LTV) and pipeline opportunities.
"MoonCliq's tailored Zoho CRM and custom application gave us complete command over our client journeys. Our communication is structured, our retainers have expanded, and our customer retention has scaled significantly." — The OZ Studio Leadership Team, Mumbai
