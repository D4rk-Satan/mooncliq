In plain English

We collect only what we need to reply to you and deliver projects. We never sell your data. You can ask us to access, correct or delete it at any time at support@mooncliq.com.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Kshiv, operating as MoonCliq (“we”, “us”), handles personal data. It covers our website mooncliq.com, our Zoho, software and WhatsApp automation services, and our product moonCliq CRM at crm.mooncliq.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry details: your name, work email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, service interest and message, when you use a form, email, WhatsApp or a call.
  • Project information: business details and access you share so we can deliver a project, such as spreadsheets or Zoho accounts.
  • Billing details: company name, GSTIN or tax details and invoices. Payments go through certified providers such as Razorpay or Stripe, and we do not store full card numbers.
  • Account data: information you provide when you register for moonCliq CRM.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to enquiries, send quotes and plan projects.
  • To deliver, support and improve our services and moonCliq CRM.
  • To invoice you, keep statutory accounting records and meet legal duties.
  • To keep our systems secure and prevent misuse.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it.
  • To send updates or marketing, only where you have agreed or the law allows. You can opt out at any time.

Where your local law requires a legal basis (for example, UK or EU data protection law), we rely on your consent, performing a contract with you, our legitimate interests in running our business, or legal obligations.

4. Our role: controller and processor

Data we control. For enquiry, billing and account data, we decide why and how it is used, so we act as the data fiduciary or controller.

Data we process for clients. When we work inside a client system, or when you use moonCliq CRM, your business records and customer data belong to you. We process them only on your instructions, and we do not inspect, change or disclose them except as needed to deliver the service or as required by law.

5. moonCliq CRM and WhatsApp

moonCliq CRM uses Meta’s official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Phone numbers, template variables and message content are sent to Meta only to carry out messaging that you direct.

  • You are responsible for collecting valid opt-in consent from recipients before sending WhatsApp messages.
  • You must follow Meta’s WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.
  • You can export your data (leads, deals, accounts and custom modules) in CSV, JSON or Excel at any time.

6. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run our business, such as cloud hosting (AWS), email and communication tools, analytics, payment providers and professional advisers, and where the law requires it. These providers must protect your data.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

7. International transfers

We are based in India. Your data may be processed in India and on servers of our providers in other countries. We use contractual and technical safeguards for these transfers. If you are in the UK or EEA, contact us for details.

8. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to make the site work. We may also use analytics cookies to understand site usage, and we will ask for consent where the law requires it. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

9. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, for example tax and accounting records. For moonCliq CRM workspaces, we delete tenant data within 30 days of a verified request or termination. See our Data Deletion page.

10. Security

Data is protected with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest on AWS infrastructure. We separate tenant data and use role-based access controls. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you about incidents as the law requires.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. If you are in the UK or EEA, you may also have the right to object, restrict processing, request portability and complain to your data protection authority.

Email support@mooncliq.com. We acknowledge requests within 24 to 48 business hours and respond within the periods set by law.

12. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at people under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the “Last updated” date above. For significant changes, we will notify subscribers.
