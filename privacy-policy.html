Legal & Regulatory Framework

Privacy Policy & Data Processing Notice

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 Last Updated: October 28, 2026 Operated by: Kshiv (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) Domain: https://mooncliq.com
Regulatory Framework (DPDP Act 2023 & Meta Developer Policy):
In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and Meta Platforms, Inc. Developer Policies, this document establishes a two-part operational standard distinguishing personal data collected from direct subscribers (Part I) from enterprise CRM customer records processed on behalf of clients (Part II).

PART I — Data Collected and Controlled by Kshiv (Data Fiduciary)

This section governs personal information collected directly from individuals and authorized representatives who register, access, or subscribe to MoonCliq services and software products.

1. Information Provided Directly by Subscribers

  • Identity & Business Profile: Authorized representative’s full name, corporate email address, telephone contact, entity name, physical office address, and Tax/GSTIN credentials.
  • Billing & Transaction Details: Subscription payments and WhatsApp Cloud messaging wallet recharges executed via certified third-party payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe). Kshiv does not store or access full debit/credit card numbers or net-banking credentials on its servers.
  • Communications & Technical Support: Diagnostic logs, customer support tickets, email communications, and correspondence submitted through our service channels.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

  • To provision, authenticate, and maintain secure multi-tenant CRM workspace environments.
  • To process billing transactions, maintain statutory accounting registers, and manage subscription lifecycles.
  • To transmit mandatory transactional alerts, critical security notices, and platform maintenance advisories.
  • To identify, prevent, and remediate technical irregularities, security incidents, or policy infractions.
Zero Commercial Monetization Commitment: Kshiv does never sell, monetize, rent, or trade subscriber contact details, enterprise profiles, or organizational records to advertising exchanges, data aggregators, or external brokers.

PART II — Information Processed on Client Behalf (Data Processor Role)

This section governs all proprietary business records, lead prospects, customer communication logs, and pipeline transactions uploaded, synced, or managed within Mooncliq Orbit CRM by our subscribers.

3. Customer Data Sovereignty & Ownership

  • Client as the Data Fiduciary (Controller): The subscriber retains full and exclusive ownership of all pipeline records, deal financials, customer profiles, and communication logs hosted within their tenant workspace. The subscriber determines the lawful basis and categories of data ingested.
  • Kshiv as the Data Processor: Kshiv processes CRM data exclusively in accordance with explicit subscriber configurations, automated workflows, and the active master subscription agreement. Kshiv does not inspect, modify, or disclose tenant data except as strictly necessary to deliver computing services or as mandated by court order or applicable law.

4. Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API Obligations

As a verified Technology Solution Provider deploying Meta’s official WhatsApp Cloud API architecture:

  • Prior Express Opt-In Consent: The subscriber is exclusively responsible for securing valid, verifiable opt-in consent from end-recipients before initiating outbound WhatsApp messages via Mooncliq Orbit.
  • Confined Message Routing: Phone numbers, template variables, and message payloads are transmitted securely to Meta’s API endpoints solely to execute client-directed messaging campaigns or automated notifications.
  • Spam & Content Compliance: Subscribers must adhere strictly to Meta’s WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy. Kshiv reserves the contractual right to terminate or suspend API connectivity for any tenant engaging in verified spamming, unsolicited messaging, or policy violations.

5. Data Portability, Retention & Permanent Erasure

  • Portability Rights: Subscribers may export their complete structured datasets (Leads, Deals, Accounts, Custom Modules) at any point during active tenure in standard formats (CSV, JSON, Excel).
  • Permanent Deletion: Upon subscription termination or written request, all tenant data stored in active production databases and rotating backup systems is permanently wiped in strict accordance with statutory retention timelines.

PART III — Enterprise Security, Compliance & Governance

6. Security Architecture & Data Isolation

Kshiv implements defense-in-depth infrastructure safeguards to protect tenant assets: all data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption, and stored data is safeguarded with AES-256 cryptographic standards hosted on enterprise AWS cloud architecture. Strict tenant database separation and granular Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC) ensure total workspace isolation.

7. Statutory Grievance Redressal Officer

In accordance with Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Rule 5(9) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the designated Grievance & Data Protection Officer is:

Legal Entity Kshiv
Product / Platform MoonCliq & Mooncliq Orbit
Jurisdiction & City Surat, Gujarat, India
Official Support Desk support@mooncliq.com

All regulatory notices and user grievances submitted to the Grievance Desk are acknowledged within 24–48 business hours and resolved within statutory periods prescribed under Indian law.

8. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy and any related contractual obligations or disputes shall be governed by, interpreted, and enforced strictly in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. The parties submit to the exclusive territorial jurisdiction of the competent courts in Surat, Gujarat, India.
