Data Deletion Instructions

How to remove your data and revoke access to moonCliq CRM, in line with Meta’s Platform Policy and India’s DPDP Act, 2023.

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Operated by Kshiv (MoonCliq)

On this page

In plain English

You can remove Facebook access yourself in a minute, or email support@mooncliq.com to delete your workspace and data. We reply within 24 to 48 business hours and delete within 30 days.

Method 1: Remove access on Facebook (instant)

If you connected your WhatsApp Business Account or Facebook Login through moonCliq CRM, you can remove access yourself:

  1. Log in to Facebook and go to Settings & privacy > Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites.
  3. Find mooncliqcrm in your connected apps.
  4. Click Remove, and optionally tick the box to delete content the app published for you.
  5. Confirm. Facebook then tells our system to invalidate your tokens.

Method 2: Ask us to delete your data

To delete your moonCliq CRM workspace, contacts, leads and message logs, or your enquiry and project data, email us:

To: support@mooncliq.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request – [your registered email or domain]

Include: your organisation name, the administrator email, and a clear statement of what you want deleted.

We may ask you to verify your identity before we delete anything.

What happens next

  • Acknowledgement: within 24 to 48 business hours, with a tracking reference.
  • Access removed: Meta API tokens, phone number IDs and webhook subscriptions are disconnected immediately.
  • Deletion: workspace databases, contacts and cached media are permanently deleted within 30 calendar days.
  • Confirmation: we email you when deletion is complete.

What we may keep

We keep a minimum of records only where the law requires it, such as tax invoices and statutory audit logs. We do not use them for any other purpose.
