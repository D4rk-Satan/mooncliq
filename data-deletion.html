Compliance & Data Rights

User Data Deletion Instructions

Legal Entity: Kshiv Framework: Meta Platform Policy & DPDP Act 2023 Response Time: 24–48 Business Hours Platform: MoonCliq & Mooncliq Orbit CRM
User Right to Erasure: In strict compliance with the Meta Platform Developer Policies and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India, MoonCliq ensures that users and enterprise clients retain full authority over their personal information and third-party account authorizations.

Mooncliq Orbit CRM does not retain any Platform Data beyond what is strictly necessary to provide CRM functionality and WhatsApp communication workflows. You can request deletion of your account records or revoke Meta authorizations through the following channels:

Method 1 — Revoking App Permissions on Facebook (Instant Self-Service)

If you authenticated your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) or Facebook Login via Mooncliq Embedded Signup, you can revoke access and remove platform data directly via your Facebook account:

Facebook Self-Service Steps
  1. Log into your Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar menu, select Apps and Websites (or visit facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).
  3. Locate mooncliqcrm in your connected applications list.
  4. Click the Remove button adjacent to the app name.
  5. Optionally select the checkbox to delete all posts, videos, or events that mooncliqcrm published on your behalf.
  6. Click Remove to confirm. Facebook will automatically dispatch a revocation webhook to our cloud infrastructure to invalidate all tokens.

Method 2 — Requesting Complete Workspace Deletion via Support Desk

To request permanent deletion of your Mooncliq Orbit CRM organization data, leads, contacts, and WhatsApp message logs from our AWS servers, email our Grievance Desk:

Email Deletion Request

Email: support@mooncliq.com

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email or Domain]

Details to Include: Your organization name, registered administrator email address, and confirmation that you wish to execute a full tenant data purge.

Data Deletion SLA & Retention Protocols

  • Acknowledgment: Our privacy compliance team will acknowledge receipt of your written request within 24 to 48 business hours with a unique deletion tracking ticket.
  • Access Invalidation: Active Meta Graph API tokens, phone number IDs, and WABA webhook subscriptions are immediately detached from your organization.
  • Data Purge: Tenant database partitions, contact repositories, and cached media files stored on AWS servers are permanently purged within 30 calendar days.
  • Statutory Archival: Minimal transactional records (such as tax invoices or statutory audit logs) are retained solely to the extent mandated by applicable Indian tax and corporate laws.
